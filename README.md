BakeBook 🍰

A personal recipe journal for home bakers.

Save your recipes, upload photos, organize your baking creations, and explore recipes shared by others.

Features
- Create an account and personal profile
- Add, edit, and delete recipes
- Upload recipe photos
- Keep recipes private or share them publicly
- Search and explore recipes
- Mobile-friendly design
